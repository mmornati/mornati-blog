RECIPE: electric water heater on solar surplus and off-peak hours (Home Assistant)
Source: https://blog.mornati.net/smart-water-heater-orchestration-solar-off-peak-v2/

Help me build this automation in MY Home Assistant. Start from my devices, not from someone else's code.

BEFORE WRITING ANYTHING
1. Find my water heaters: the relay switching each one and its power sensor.
2. Find my solar production and house consumption sensors, and check whether house consumption includes the heater.
3. List the automations that already switch the heaters.
4. Ask me, then wait for my answers: power of each heater, my off-peak windows, how I flag that we are away, how to notify me.
No access to my Home Assistant? Ask instead of assuming.

THE PROBLEM
A heater on a fixed off-peak schedule does not know whether the tank is really hot, nor whether the sun could finish the job for free. Guessing costs money once there is solar.

WHAT I WANT (for each heater)
A. End of cycle: the heater's own thermostat declares it. When power collapses, cut the relay, mark the tank finished, notify me.
B. Solar run: if the tank is not finished when the daytime off-peak window closes, keep heating at peak rate only if the solar surplus covers the whole heater.
C. Watch: during a solar run, stop when the sun drops or the house load spikes. The tank stays "not finished".
D. Night: a tank still not finished gets a night cycle, ended by the thermostat or by a time cap, whichever comes first.

STARTING THRESHOLDS (calibrate them at my home)
- Finished: power below 50 W for 5 minutes.
- Surplus: production minus house >= heater power x 1.1, with house below 1500 W.
- Abort: house above 1500 W for 2 minutes.
- Night: 02:00, cap of 90 minutes (large tank) or 60 (small).

SAFEGUARDS
- Never import at peak rate to heat water.
- Heater meter unavailable for 10 minutes: cut the relay and end the cycle.
- Solar or house sensor unavailable: no solar run.
- Away mode on: nothing starts.
- The watch only touches solar runs.
- Only switch the relay, never the tank's setpoint.

TEST CASES (from the article)
1. Heater under 50 W for 5 minutes -> relay off, tank finished, notification.
2. Meter unavailable for 10 minutes -> relay off, cycle ended.
3. 14:09, tank not finished, 2.9 kW heater, surplus >= 3190 W, house under 1500 W -> solar run starts.
4. Same with a smaller surplus -> nothing, the night handles it.
5. Solar run, house above 1500 W for 2 minutes (oven, kettle) -> relay off, tank still "not finished".
6. 02:00, tank not finished, thermostat never cuts -> stop at 90 minutes.
7. Away mode on -> no solar run, no night cycle.

METHOD
First a short plan and your questions. Then the automations with my entities, each threshold explained, and a way to test before enabling. Start with end-of-cycle detection alone.
