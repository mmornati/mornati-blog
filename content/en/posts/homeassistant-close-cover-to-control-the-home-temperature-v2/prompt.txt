RECIPE: closing shutters by zone when it is hot (Home Assistant)
Source: https://blog.mornati.net/homeassistant-close-cover-to-control-the-home-temperature-v2/

You are going to help me build this automation in MY Home Assistant. Do not copy someone else's code: start from my devices.

BEFORE WRITING ANYTHING
1. List my shutters, roof windows included: which accept a position, which only open and close.
2. Find my temperature sensors, outdoor and indoor, room by room.
3. Look at which automations already move these shutters, and when.
4. Ask me your questions, then wait for my answers: which windows get the sun and when, how to group them into zones, how far to close, when to reopen.
If you have no access to my Home Assistant, ask me instead of assuming.

THE PROBLEM
In summer the sun heats the house through the windows. Each side gets the sun at a different time, and a single temperature sensor is too fragile to decide when to close.

WHAT I WANT
A. Averaged temperatures: one outdoor average, one indoor average per floor, each built from several sensors.
B. One closing rule per zone, checked every 10 minutes inside that zone's time window, comparing outdoor and indoor.
C. One reopening rule per zone, later in the day, acting only if that zone's closing rule ran the same day.
D. Each rule acts at most once a day.

STARTING THRESHOLDS (to calibrate at my home)
- Afternoon zone (ground floor): 12:00-18:00, outdoor average more than 2 °C above indoor average, reference room above 20 °C, position 40. Reopen 19:00-20:00.
- Morning zone (office): 08:00-13:00, outdoor simply above indoor, room above 20 °C, position 40. Reopen 14:00-14:30.
- Roof windows: 11:00-18:00, outdoor average above first floor average, full closure. Reopen 19:00-20:00.
- "Already done": a rule never runs twice within 8 hours.

SAFEGUARDS (not negotiable)
- One sensor missing from an average breaks nothing: the average carries on with the others.
- Every sensor of an average unavailable or unknown: do nothing.
- Never reopen a zone the automation did not close itself that day.
- Shutters it closed must not stay closed all night.

TEST CASES (rules from the article)
1. Ground floor, 12:00-18:00, outdoor more than 2 °C above indoor, reference room above 20 °C -> position 40.
2. Same, outdoor warmer by less than 2 °C -> nothing (the margin avoids false triggers on cloudy days).
3. Same, reference room at 20 °C or below -> nothing.
4. Office, 08:00-13:00, outdoor just above indoor, room above 20 °C -> position 40, no margin.
5. Roof windows, after 11:00, outdoor above first floor average -> full closure, not 40.
6. Conditions still true 10 minutes after a closure -> no second command.
7. 19:00-20:00: zone closed by the automation that day -> reopens; zone it did not close -> nothing.
8. One sensor of a group unavailable -> the rule still works.

METHOD
First a short plan and your questions. Then the automations with my entities, an explanation of each threshold, and a way to test before enabling: start with one zone, with a notification in place of the shutter command.
