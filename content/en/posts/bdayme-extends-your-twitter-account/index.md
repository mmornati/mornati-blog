I'm one of the few people who prefer to use [Twitter](http://www.twitter.com) as my personal social network than [Facebook](http://www.facebook.com). It gives you an anonymous way to follow people that could be really useful if you want, for example, just get information from a journalist or magazine.

## Overview

But, starting today, if you want to add a *social touch* to your twitter account, you can use this new service, offered (app is completely free) by a group of Italian guys: [BDayMe](http://www.bdayme.com/).

[![](/images/bdayme-extends-your-twitter-account/00-Schermata-05-2456079-alle-22_07_33_kfpqct.png)](https://res.cloudinary.com/blog-mornati-net/image/upload/v1391641342/Schermata-05-2456079-alle-22_07_33_kfpqct.png)

## How It Works

The app is really simple: you can install it from the AppStore (for the moment App is available just for iOS devices, but other devices will be compatible in the future), connect using your twitter account, and... you are **in**.
Then you can set an automatic message that bdayme server will tweet for you the day of your birthday; see the birthday of your contacts and send them wishes. As I said you add a social touch to your twitter account.

But, if you want to know when your preferred V.I.P. birthday is and be the first to greet them on their birthday... you **must** install bdayme on your device.
The app is completely free, so... you can make a little test.
