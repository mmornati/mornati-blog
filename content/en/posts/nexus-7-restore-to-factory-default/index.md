# Nexus 7: restore to factory default

Even if I never "hacked" my Nexus device, after a few months (I suppose due to application installation) my device crashed and, after reboot, I was stuck on the Google boot image.
I tried leaving it to boot for an hour with no success. So... factory reset.
Yes, but how do I factory reset a crashed device? Reading online I found forum posts with combinations that I honestly didn't understand.

Here is the combination I tested many times to wipe your device:

1. Reboot your device by pressing **Power Button** + **Sound *Down* Button** at the same time until you see the fast boot menu
   [![](/images/nexus-7-restore-to-factory-default/00-and.png)](http://www.r33net.de/wp-uploads/2012/11/and.png)
2. Use the volume buttons to move to *Recovery Mode* menu and then start it using the power button
3. Your Nexus 7 will reboot and, after a while, you should see the Android robot with a red *exclamation mark* and the information *no data* on the screen
4. Press **Power Button** + **Sound Up Button** at the *same time* for about **2 seconds** and then release the two buttons at the same time. The recovery menu should appear
5. Use the volume buttons to navigate to the *Wipe Data* menu (and if you want *Wipe Cache*)
6. At the end, select the *Reboot* entry in the menu to restart your device

Hope this helps!
