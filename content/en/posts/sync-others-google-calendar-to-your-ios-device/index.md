## Overview

By default, when you configure a Google account on your iOS device, only your default calendar gets synchronized with iPhone/iPad. But if you have, like me, configured and imported some other calendars that are displayed on your gcal, you surely would have them on your portable device too.

For example, in my account, I've imported my Google Apps (professional) calendar, French/Italian holidays calendar, and some others.

## The Problem

To configure what you want to have on your iOS device too, you should visit the mobile sync page (from your device):

[http://m.google.com/sync](http://m.google.com/sync)

If you have your Google account with a language different from English (I just tested with French and Italian, so maybe some other languages work without this tip), you will see an "error" page:

[![Error page - Google Sync not supported on your device](/images/sync-others-google-calendar-to-your-ios-device/00-Photo-10-05-12-23-04-31_x4dgen.png)](https://res.cloudinary.com/blog-mornati-net/image/upload/v1391641352/Photo-10-05-12-23-04-31_x4dgen.png)

The error page says *google sync is not supported on your device*. And even if you retry to refresh the url, you always get the error. As we already (partially) said, the problem is not your device but the language used to show the page.

## The Solution (Language Workaround)

If you click on "change language" (naturally the link is displayed in your language, in the image it is "modifica lingua") and select **English US** you can see the real sync page.
