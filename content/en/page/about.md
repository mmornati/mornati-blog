Hi! I'm **Marco Mornati**, a developer who enjoys building software, tinkering with self-hosted
infrastructure, and writing about it.

This blog used to live on Hashnode; it now lives here, self-hosted on my own VPS, built with
[Hugo](https://gohugo.io) and the [Blowfish](https://blowfish.page/) theme, and published through
plain **git + CI/CD** — because the best way to own your content is to keep it in a repository.

If you want to reach me, the fastest way is [GitHub](https://github.com/mmornati).
