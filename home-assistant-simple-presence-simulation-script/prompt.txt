RECIPE: presence simulation with lights while the house is empty (Home Assistant)
Source: https://blog.mornati.net/home-assistant-simple-presence-simulation-script/

You are going to help me build this automation in MY Home Assistant. Do not copy someone else's code: start from my devices.

BEFORE WRITING ANYTHING
1. List my lights and other simple on/off devices, room by room.
2. Find what tells Home Assistant that nobody is home: an alarm in away mode, an "away" toggle, presence tracking.
3. Check which automations already switch these lights.
4. Ask me your questions, then wait for my answers: which lights can be seen from outside, how I declare that I am away, when the simulation should stop for the night.
If you have no access to my Home Assistant, ask me instead of assuming.

THE PROBLEM
An empty house stays dark all evening, and it shows from the street. I want it to look lived in: lights that come on and go off after dark, with no fixed pattern.

WHAT I WANT
A. A reusable routine: turn a given light on, wait a given duration, turn that same light off.
B. A regular check, not a fixed start time. With a fixed time, leaving five minutes later means nothing ever runs.
C. At each check, if I am away and it is dark, pick one light and one duration at random.
D. Several lights may be on at once, each with its own timer.

STARTING THRESHOLDS (to calibrate at my home)
- Check every 30 minutes.
- Start 30 minutes before sunset.
- No new light after 23:59.
- Duration: random, 5 to 30 minutes.
- List: about four lights visible from outside.

SAFEGUARDS (not negotiable)
- A new run must never cancel a timer still running, or that light never goes off.
- Only lights from my list.
- The duration must always be a valid delay: a badly formatted value makes the wait fail.
- Away state unknown or unavailable: do nothing.

TEST CASES
1. Away, dark, half-hour check -> one listed light comes on, then goes off 5 to 30 minutes later.
2. Not away, dark -> nothing.
3. Away, more than 30 minutes before sunset -> nothing.
4. Away, 20 minutes before sunset -> a light may come on.
5. I leave at 20:05 -> the simulation starts at the next half-hour check.
6. A second light is picked while the first is still on -> both stay on, each goes off at its own time.
7. Drawn duration of 5 minutes -> the light goes off after 5 minutes.
8. Check after 23:59 -> no new light.

METHOD
First give me a short plan and your questions. Then write the routine and the automation with my entities, explain each threshold, and show me how to test before enabling: run the routine by hand on one light with a short duration, and check that it goes off.
